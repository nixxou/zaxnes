# V2-24 — Enhanced style: AMD-055 v1.4 (local-camera floor, AO ceiling)

Lot V2-24 (R3, clean side). AMD-055 goes to **v1.4** and closes both tickets of lot V2-23:

- **Q-096 → answered.** The provisional choice is right for the ordinary camera (`+Y`, close-up R
  unchanged), but the camera's vertical is wrong in local-camera mode too: the "Punch Out!!"
  profile of 3dSen 2.0 tilts its local camera by **−60°**, where
  `dot((0, 0, −1), q · (0, 1, 0))` = 0.866 < 0.9, and the reference still reflects the mat.
  **In local-camera mode the vertical is the image-plane normal facing the camera**, `(0, 0, −1)`
  in the scene frame before curvature, whatever the tilt; the extent is measured in the `xy`
  plane.
- **Q-097 → answered.** Hypothesis 1 confirmed: the reference is **not** under the
  `0.1 × ao.strength` ceiling. The ceiling becomes `O ≤ min(0.95 ; 0.3 × ao.strength)` (0.9 by
  default) and the contact invariant of close-up A becomes « strictly below 1 and ≥ **0.10** », to
  one sRGB level. Recalibrate both modes on the Q-095 reference figures (area **and** mean
  darkening), the band staying soft — continuous fall-off, but deep at contact.

## Notes

### Measurement method (V2-23's, unchanged)

Captures downscaled to 960 × 540 (box filter). Two figures per capture:

- **zone** = share of pixels whose sum over RGB of |effect − base| exceeds 12;
- **mean darkening** = mean over the whole 960 × 540 image and the three channels of
  (base − effect), in 0-255 levels.

Scenes: Punch-Out!! (`Mike Tyson's Punch-O [Japan][USA][!!].nes`, profile "Punch Out!!",
`--run 1501` with the lot's start presses) and Super Mario Bros. (`[Europe][!]`, `--run 419`),
both `--size 2560x1440 --style 4`. The comparison tool lives in the session scratchpad.

(The zone/mean figures reproduce V2-23's within the rounding of the downscale; the ratio columns
are new.)

**What the ratio columns say, and it is the whole of Q-097's answer in one line.** Both modes read
a darkest 1 % of **0.69** and a darkest 5 % of **0.70** — the signature of a population *clipped*
against the old ceiling `0.1 × ao.strength` = 0.3, not of a distribution. The reference, at the
same settings, reads 0.13 / 0.42 (Depth) and 0.16 / 0.35 (MSVO): it goes **five times deeper at
the contact** than the old ceiling even permits. Raising the ceiling to
`min(0.95 ; 0.3 × ao.strength)` = 0.9 is therefore not a free parameter to be spent on more area —
it has to be spent on **depth**, and the gain of each mode has to come *down* by roughly the same
factor so that the area does not run away with it. The two figures to land are
`under 0.70` ≈ 11.8 % (Depth) / 19.8 % (MSVO) and `darkest 1 %` ≈ 0.13 / 0.16.

Framing-adjusted targets (×1.23 on area, and on the whole-image mean, which scales with it):
Depth **28.7 % / 6.4**, MSVO **39.6 % / 8.7**.
