# V2-25 — Enhanced style: reflection strength/roughness and MSVO large scales (AMD-055 v1.4)

Lot V2-25 (R3, clean side). The R5 black-box verification of `0324e44` reports two defects of the
Enhanced style, both on REQ-SCN-083/084 (P3, « revue »):

- **Defect A** — the floor reflections are about **twice too strong** and too clean. Zone right
  (reference 10.5 % of the frame, Zaxnes 11.4 %, 61 % overlap), but inside the zone the mean
  RGB-summed |effect − base| is **63** for the reference against **122** here; the signed
  (brightening) mean 38 against 73. The reference looks broken up and faded, this one is a clean
  mirror.
- **Defect B** — the MSVO mode **lacks its large-scale component**: the reference darkens broadly
  (mean darkening ≈ **15.7** levels over the mat centre, region 380-580 × 300-420 of a 960 × 540
  frame), this one concentrates in bands along edges and contacts (≈ **11.2**) and its zone is
  smaller (23 % against 32 %, overlap 62 %).

### The Punch-Out!! ROM is gone

« Punch Out!! », `--run 1501`) and on « Super Mario Bros. » 1-1. **That ROM is no longer on this

`test-originals/` (98 files) holds no Punch-Out!! It cannot be the R5 scene for this lot, and the
reference's own figures for it cannot be reproduced here.

What is left, and what this lot measures on:

- **Close-up R** and **close-up A** of AMD-055 §2.4 and §2.5 — the normative, third-party
  reproducible configurations, which the acceptance fixtures already build. Both defects are
  measurable on them: defect A is a magnitude at default settings, defect B a spatial extent.
- **Super Mario Bros.** 1-1 (`Super Mario Bros. [Europe][!].nes`, `--run 419`), which is still
  here and which R5 measured too (reflection zone 1.1 % reference against 1.3 % here).
- A **synthetic MSVO scene** for the large-scale contribution, which is what the lot asks the
  regression test to cover: the floor of close-up A with a **kerb** of 64 × 8 × 8 units across it
  in place of the close-up's single cube. The occluder has to be *laterally large* for the
  question to mean anything — the port's pattern is a seven-entry checkerboard scaled by the
  sphere radius of its level, so a coarse level samples sparsely in absolute terms and an 8-unit
  cube falls between its samples whatever the pyramid does.

Measurement method, unchanged from V2-23/V2-24 where it applies: captures downscaled to
960 × 540 by a box filter; « zone » = share of pixels whose sum over RGB of |effect − base|
exceeds 12; « mean » over the whole image and the three channels.

Two figures are added for this lot, the ones R5 reports:

- **in-zone mean** = mean over the pixels of the zone of the RGB-summed |effect − base|
  (defect A: 63 reference, 122 here);
- **in-zone signed mean** = the same, signed, (effect − base) (defect A: 38 reference, 73 here).

### Step 2 — defect A: the audit, and what it leaves

**The decisive measurement.** The cube of the close-up R is *pure red*, so the reflected colour
has no green at all and a floor pixel's green comes out at exactly `(1 − i)` of what it was. Read
at the two contact pixels of the first invariant of §2.5, in linear:

`1 − V_on / V_off` = **0.448** and **0.448** — that is `i` = 0.45 to under one sRGB level, which
is exactly `0,09 × n(3)` and exactly the mix `(1 − i) · surface + i · reflet` the Note of §2.5
writes, **applied once** (twice would read 0.70).

That single figure excludes four of the six causes the lot lists — the mix formula, mixing
something other than the reflected lit colour, a double application, and a half-resolution trace
upsampled without the blur (the realised weight is exact to the pixel). The other two:

- **the blur at the default clarity** *is* applied, but the text itself makes it invisible near a
  contact: the radius is `0,003 × (10 − n(ssr.clarity))` of the screen height **at the maximum
  length** and **proportional to the distance travelled**, so at `ssr.length` = 3 (200 units) a
  contact reflection of 1 to 30 units gets 0.1 to 3 pixels at 1080p, not the 16 the setting
  reaches at 200 units. The text asks for a clean mirror at a contact, and that is what it gets;
- **the attenuation** *was* applied, but only over the **last quarter** of the ray
  (`1 − smoothstep(0.75, 1, t/L)`), which at the default length atones for nothing before 150
  units — nothing at all in a game frame. **This is the one real deviation**, against the
  *Intention* (« s'estompe avec la distance parcourue ») and the review criterion (« se brouille
  et s'efface avec la distance »), and it is fixed: the fall-off now spans the whole ray,
  `1 − smoothstep(0, 1, t/L)`, which the freedom left on « la largeur et la forme de ces deux
  transitions » allows.

| figure | before | **after** |
|---|---|---|
| close-up R, in-zone mean | 116.17 | **116.12** |
| SMB 1-1, in-zone mean | 90.57 | **88.32** |
| SMB 1-1, zone | 1.035 % | 1.034 % |

The correction is worth 2.5 % on SMB 1-1 and nothing on the close-up R, because in both the
reflected geometry sits close to the floor: the factor of 1.9 R5 measures is **not** distance
dependent. The implementation now follows §2.5 to the letter and is still about twice the
reference, so — as the lot instructs — **[Q-099](../../../spec/changes/Q-099.md)** is opened
rather than `0,09` retuned. Its finding: the reference's figures and its *fragmented* look have a
single common explanation, a **slightly rough floor** (the reflected ray scattered per pixel, so
part of the rays reach nothing), which halves the mean inside the zone without moving the zone or
the mix weight, and which no « forme du noyau de flou » can produce — a blur conserves the mean
it spreads. The ratio |Δ| / signed is 1.66 on both sides, which a coverage factor preserves and a
different mix formula would not.

### Step 3 — defect B: four real departures from the original, and the one that matters

The four, each checkable against `third_party/MiniEngine-SSAO/`:

1. **The `SmartBlur` was missing entirely.** The original blurs its low-resolution input at
   **every** step of `BlurAndUpsample` (±2 texels, `((a + e)/2 + b + c + d)/4`) and ends with one
   more step at full resolution, so **no** level reaches the output unblurred; the port multiplied
   each level's own occlusion in raw, leaving the finest level — the one that carries the contact
   — completely unsmoothed. It is also the « lissage qui respecte les discontinuités de
   profondeur » §2.4 asks for. Added as two separable passes per level (`MsvoShaders.BlurFragment`,
   `MsvoPass.Smooth`), the boolean `CompareDeltas` of the original becoming a continuous weight on
   the relative depth difference (no group-shared cache in OpenGL 3.3).
2. **The pyramid had 3 levels** where the original's `HierarchyDepth` allows **4**.
3. **The coarsest sphere was `r`**, which gave the mode an influence radius *below* the Depth
   mode's; it is now **1.75 r** — what the `2 r` far field of §2.4 leaves, less the reach of the
   smoothing — the darkening dying by 37 units against the 40 the invariant measures at.
4. **The pattern was fixed per pixel** where the original samples a **deinterleaved** depth
   (sixteen sub-grids), so neighbouring pixels probe different directions and the blur averages
   them. Now turned by a per-pixel hashed angle, which REQ-SCN-083 permits in as many words.

**This does not close the gap**, and the measurement says why. With the two coarse levels alone
(spheres of 35 and 17.5) the kerb's 8-12 band reads **5.54**; with all four levels, **5.47** —
identical. The mid-field comes entirely from the coarse levels, and they find 5.5 levels where the
Depth hemisphere finds 25.5. So it is neither a missing level, nor a blend that drowns them, nor
too short a reach: it is the **sensitivity** of the ported estimator.

The cause, and it is structural: `AoRenderCS.hlsli` compares a sample's depth to the **centre
pixel's**, and the opposite-pair construction cancels the surface's own depth gradient — which is
exactly what leaves a lone plane at 1.00 (§2.4's first invariant) and exactly what makes an
occluder count only by its *departure from that gradient* rather than by its **rise above the
tangent plane**. At 10 units from an 8-unit-tall kerb the truly blocked share of the hemisphere is
≈ 20 %; the MiniEngine estimator finds 8 %, the Depth estimator (which reconstructs a normal)
33 %. Closing it means changing estimator, and *Mode MSVO* **names** the one to port. Hence
**[Q-100](../../../spec/changes/Q-100.md)**.

`zaxMsvoShare` is deliberately **not** retouched. R5's two figures are both in a ratio of 1.4
(mat centre 11.2 against 15.7, zone 23 % against 32 %), which a plain gain would give since the
ceiling `O ≤ 0.9` clips the contact and lets the mid-field grow alone — but the Punch-Out!! ROM
those figures come from is gone from this machine, so that would be tuning blind. The sensitivity
is measured instead and put in Q-100: 0.75 → 0.54 gives **+43 %** on the kerb's 8-12 band
(5.47 → 7.80). One constant closes it for whoever can measure the reference scene.

All thirteen §2.4 invariants of `Amd055OcclusionTests` still pass.
