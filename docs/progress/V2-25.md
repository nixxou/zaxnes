# V2-25 — Enhanced style: reflection strength/roughness and MSVO large scales (AMD-055 v1.4)

Lot V2-25 (R3, clean side). The R5 black-box verification of `0324e44` reports two defects of the
Enhanced style, both on REQ-SCN-083/084 (P3, « revue »):

- **Defect A** — the floor reflections are about **twice too strong** and too clean. Zone right
  (reference 10.5 % of the frame, Zaxnes 11.4 %, 61 % overlap), but inside the zone the mean
  RGB-summed |effect − base| is **63** for the reference against **122** here; the signed
  (brightening) mean 38 against 73. The reference looks broken up and faded, this one is a clean
  mirror.
- **Defect B** — the MSVO mode **lacks its large-scale component**: the reference darkens broadly
  (mean darkening ≈ **15.7** levels over the mat centre, region 380-580 × 300-420 of a 960 × 540
  frame), this one concentrates in bands along edges and contacts (≈ **11.2**) and its zone is
  smaller (23 % against 32 %, overlap 62 %).

## Notes

### The Punch-Out!! ROM is gone

V2-22 to V2-24 measured on « Punch-Out!! » (`Mike Tyson's Punch-O [Japan][USA][!!].nes`, profile
« Punch Out!! », `--run 1501`) and on « Super Mario Bros. » 1-1. **That ROM is no longer on this
machine**: `find /g/3dsen -iname '*Punch*.nes'` and `-iname '*Tyson*'` both come back empty, and
`test-originals/` (98 files) holds no Punch-Out!! It cannot be the R5 scene for this lot, and the
reference's own figures for it cannot be reproduced here.

What is left, and what this lot measures on:

- **Close-up R** and **close-up A** of AMD-055 §2.4 and §2.5 — the normative, third-party
  reproducible configurations, which the acceptance fixtures already build. Both defects are
  measurable on them: defect A is a magnitude at default settings, defect B a spatial extent.
- **Super Mario Bros.** 1-1 (`Super Mario Bros. [Europe][!].nes`, `--run 419`), which is still
  here and which R5 measured too (reflection zone 1.1 % reference against 1.3 % here).
- A **synthetic MSVO scene** (floor + cube, the geometry of close-up A) for the large-scale
  contribution, which is what the lot asks the regression test to cover.

Measurement method, unchanged from V2-23/V2-24 where it applies: captures downscaled to
960 × 540 by a box filter; « zone » = share of pixels whose sum over RGB of |effect − base|
exceeds 12; « mean » over the whole image and the three channels.

Two figures are added for this lot, the ones R5 reports:

- **in-zone mean** = mean over the pixels of the zone of the RGB-summed |effect − base|
  (defect A: 63 reference, 122 here);
- **in-zone signed mean** = the same, signed, (effect − base) (defect A: 38 reference, 73 here).

### Step 1 — before figures, measured here at `0324e44`

The measuring fixture is `tests/Acceptance/Amd055StyleStrengthTests.cs`. It is **carried
uncommitted through steps 2 and 3** and lands with its bounds in step 4, so that every commit of
this lot leaves the suite green; if this session is cut, keep the file (it only measures) and
continue at the first unchecked step.

**Close-up R**, 1920 × 1080, `spl.reflections` = 1, default `ssr.*`:

| figure | before |
|---|---|
| zone (share of the frame) | **1.330 %** |
| in-zone mean of the RGB-summed abs. change | **116.2** |
| in-zone signed mean | +11.0 |

The in-zone mean reproduces the defect's own figure almost exactly — R5 measured **122** against
the reference's 63 on « Punch-Out!! », and this normative fixture reads 116 — so the close-up R
carries defect A faithfully even though its absolute level is its own (a pure red cube one unit
from a dark floor). The signed mean is small *here* and only here: the red channel rises by what
the cube reflects while the green and the blue fall by the `(1 − i)` the mix leaves the surface,
and on a monochrome fixture the two very nearly cancel in the sum.

**Close-up A**, MSVO, default `ao.*` — the mean darkening of the floor in sRGB levels, by
distance from the cube's footprint (the scene's only occluder), which is the profile defect B is
about:

| band (scene units) | 0-4 | 4-8 | 8-12 | 12-16 | 16-20 | 20-24 | 24-28 | 28-32 | 32-48 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| before | **30.33** | 13.78 | 7.05 | 3.83 | 1.76 | 0.79 | 0.47 | 0.11 | 0.00 |

Summary figures: contact band (< 0.25 r = 5 units) **30.33**, wide band (0.5 r to r, 10 to 20
units) **4.21**, beyond 2 r **0.00**; wide / contact **0.139**. The profile halves about every
four units — the signature of a *single* small scale, not of a multi-scale estimate, and the
measurable form of « concentrated in bands along edges/contacts ».
