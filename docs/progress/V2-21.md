## Notes

- The style and its twelve parameters are **global user settings**, never taken from the profile
  (AMD-055 REQ-SCN-078). The "profile" layer of the precedence the lot asks for is therefore the
  one a profile **script** can write through `Setting.VisualStyle` and
  `Setting:SetVisualStyleParameter` (REQ-LUA-114), which lands in the session layer of the
  settings model (REQ-PERS-010). The CLI options are applied after the run, last.
- Accepted style ids: `0`/`classic`, `4`/`enhanced`, and `1` as the alias of Enhanced that
  REQ-SCN-078 accepts in writing and reads back as 4.
- Parameter keys: the twelve Lua identifiers of REQ-SCN-079 (`spl.ao`, `ao.strength`, `ao.radius`,
  `ao.thickness`, `ao.quality`, `spl.pixel_shadow`, `spl.voxel_grid`, `spl.reflections`,
  `ssr.shine`, `ssr.clarity`, `ssr.length`, `ssr.quality`), the stable `render.style.*` keys being
  accepted as well. The four curvature parameters (`spl.bend_*`, `spl.arc_*`) are **not** style
  parameters (AMD-043 acts in both styles): `--bend` keeps them.
- `tests/Acceptance` is reserved for the round-1 verifier (R4); the new tests go to
  `tests/Zax.Harness.Tests`, the acceptance suite is only **run**.
- The lot's examples `spl.ssr` and `voxel.grid` are not identifiers of REQ-SCN-079; the lot also
  asks for "the exact keys of the amendment", which are `spl.reflections` and `spl.voxel_grid`.
  Those are the ones accepted; the other two spellings are refused with the list of accepted
  keys, so no question ticket was needed.
- Final state: `Zax.Harness.Tests` 127/127 in Release, `Zax.Acceptance.Tests` filtered to
  render/scene/settings 273/273 and, on the Amd055/Render/Setting/Scn filter, 193 passed with
  one skipped (`Q062StressTests`, a load test). Whole solution builds with no warning.
