  `PatternVariant` gained `TextureKind` / `XorTexture`; the decoder and the encoder took a hash
  mode. 22 new tests in `HashProfileReaderTests`, Profiles suite 282/282.
- **Correction to a reading of REQ-PROF-074**: the four groups of the 101-file criterion add up
  to the 74 482 variants — 60 777 of `textureKind = 1` and **13 705** of `textureKind = 2`,
  the latter split 13 559 (format 0, `tSize × 8`) + 4 (format 0, other size) + 142 (format 1).
  The sentence reads as if 13 559 were the whole of `textureKind = 2`; measured, it is the first
  subgroup only. No ticket: the arithmetic is unambiguous.
- Resolution universe = the **manifest** (`Hashes` ∪ text form of `Ids`), which is what the
  spec's counts ("481 sur 481", "173 chaînes du manifeste") are over.
- Step 2: the four vectors of REQ-PROF-075/077, the two digests of 076 and the three mirrorings
  of 079 reproduce bit for bit. ROM resolution checked on 99 synthetic iNES files and on the
  three real dumps: **481/481**, **1 035/1 035**, **5 353/5 497** exactly as the criterion says.
  Texture reconstruction on the 99 pairable profiles: 55 575 null markers + 12 418 + 4 + 130 =
  68 127 variants equal to their 3dn counterpart. Profiles suite 306/306.
- The synthetic iNES file fills the space between tiles with a deterministic xorshift filler
  rather than zeros: with zeros, a window straddling a tile and its padding happens to be a real
  tile of some profiles, and the "file without one tile" part of the criterion could not hold.
  The removed tile is also chosen so that none of its four mirrorings is another tile of the
  file (REQ-PROF-079 would resolve it from its twin). `empty.bytes` has no tile, so 98 + 1.
- Commercial dumps are found through `ZAXNES_ROMS`, as the existing ROM tests do; no machine
  path is committed (REQ-PUB-001).
- Step 3: `Cartridge.ChrRamView` / `NesCore.ChrRam` (REQ-CORE-123, 4 tests). Engine side:
  `EnginePattern` skips a pending cell and rebuilds in place (`Reload`, `ReloadFromProfile`),
  `PatternRegistry.Refresh` re-keys it, `PatternInstance` forces `Default` / no `fullWarp` while
  a tile waits, `ConversionEngine.ResolvePendingTiles` runs before the recognition of each
  displayed frame and rebuilds textures + geometry synchronously. `ResolvedTileCache` (082) under
  `<user root>/tiles`, `--no-tile-cache` on the app, `--tile-cache <dir>` on the harness (off by
  default = empty cache). Tests: Engine3D 7, App 3, Harness 2 (Mega Man 2), Nes 4.
  Suites: Nes 593, Engine3D 228, App 508, Harness 145 — all green.
- A « 3dh » payload usually carries no header field, so the envelope's Id / Title / Maker /
  GameDev / Des fill in what the payload leaves **absent** (REQ-PROF-073). A field the payload
  does carry is left alone, which keeps the byte-identical rewrite.
- **Q-178** opened: both acceptance criteria of REQ-PROF-080 are not reproducible as written.
  §1 « a tile that appears only on screen » cannot happen on a mapper 0 CHR-RAM cartridge (a
  sprite's pattern data *is* an aligned CHR-RAM block); the two paths are tested separately.
  §2 the 144 tiles of Mega Man 2 never appear in `Mega Man 2 (USA).nes` — 0 resolved over 4 000
  frames out of 3 991 348 tile records — while the mechanism checks out on that same game
  (220/220 drawn tiles found as aligned CHR-RAM blocks, 5 353/5 497 from the ROM exactly).
  Verified instead: once every tile is resolved, the 3dh capture has the same meshes, drawn tile
  by drawn tile, as the 3dn one.
