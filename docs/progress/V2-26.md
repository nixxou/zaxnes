Both of R5's figures land on the reference within 5 %, which is what the halved constant was
supposed to do. Close-up R: zone 1.323 %, in-zone mean **59.85** (116.115 before), realised mix
weight **0.218** at both contact pixels — `0,045 × n(3)` = 0.225 to under one sRGB level.

**One test needed a measurement change, not a behaviour one.**
`REQ_SCN_084_the_clarity_sharpens_the_edge_of_the_reflection` estimates the transition width as
`peak / steepest slope`, both single-pixel extrema of an 8-bit capture. Halving the amplitude
doubled the quantisation noise relative to the signal and made clarity 1 and 2 tie
(2.470 against **2.520**, a 2 % inversion) while the five widths still fell away overall
(2.470, 2.520, 2.350, 2.201, 2.048) and the blur code did not move. The fixture now raises
`ssr.shine` to 5 — the invariant names `ssr.clarity` alone — and the five widths separate again.
The loop was also restructured to measure all five first and report them together.
