### Step 3 — Part 2: where the HALFHCYLINDER `fullWarp` path deviates

**The object, read out of the engine itself** (a temporary trace on `BuildCylinder`, removed
again): the right-hand bush is one 3D object, pattern **8 × 2 cells** (64 × 16 texels),
`geo` = `HALFHCYLINDER`, `sizeZ` = 16, `solid` = `fullWarp` = **`antiShadow`** = true,
`flip` = false, `emptyColor` = 0, cells (0,0) and (7,0) empty. `--explain` names it
`p26(8x2).0`, 14 tiles, `tex 64x16`.

Two facts of the shape matter and neither is visible from the frame dump alone:

- H types cut their segments **column by column over the whole pattern**, so a full column of
  this shape is a segment of **16** pixels — `L` = 15, `A` = 30, `B` = min(15, 30) = 15,
  `D′` = 16 — crossing both cell rows. The row-192 and row-200 tiles are two halves of the same
  sections.
- `antiShadow` is **on**, which turns out to be what makes the defect visible at all.

**Everything the lot asked to re-check is correct.** The segment scan (`x` ascending, `y`
increasing within the column), `A = 2L`, `B = min(D − 1, 2L)`, `C` = `2s` with `flip` and `2f`
without, the active quadrants of REQ-E3D-447 (Q2, Q3 for `HALFHCYLINDER` without `flip`), the
axis mapping `(ligne, u, z)` of REQ-E3D-448, the empty depth span of AMD-048 and `D′` of
REQ-E3D-452 all check out against the TV-Y vectors and against a hand derivation of this shape.
The deviation is in **one** place: which texel the `fullWarp` fill reads.

**The deviation.** REQ-E3D-450 says « si `Q2` et `Q3` sont actifs, `(u⁻, z)` avec le texel de
`Q0` ». For the **full** types that names the column's **own** pixel, because REQ-E3D-447 gives
`Q0` the texel `u⁻` — which is exactly what TV-Y4 states in words. For the **half** types
REQ-E3D-447 gives each quadrant *its own position*, so `Q0` carries `u⁺`: the fill reads the
**far** side. And a half-cylinder's centre `C` sits at an **end** of the segment (REQ-E3D-445),
so the far side lies **outside** it by up to `L` pixels; REQ-E3D-426 wraps that read around the
texture and REQ-E3D-423 then makes the voxel transparent wherever it lands on an empty cell.

Worked on the bush, column `x` = 5 (segment `y` = 8..15, `C` = 30): region 1 writes at
`u⁻` = 15, 15, 14, 14, 13, 13, 12, 12, 11, 11, 10 — the whole segment — but reads at
`u⁺` = 15, 15, 16, 16, 17, 17, 18, 18, 19, 19, 20, i.e. modulo 16 the rows 0, 0, 1, 1, 2, 2,
3, 3, 4 of the **empty** cell (0,0). Only the first two survive, and (174,200) keeps the two
voxel rows `j` = 0 and 1 — the `y` 40..42 measured in step 1 instead of 40..48.

**The second argument, independent of the first.** REQ-E3D-447 says `antiShadow` substitutes
« ce choix de texel (**pas dans la position des voxels**) ». Under the letter it moves the
voxels: the `u⁻` write takes `Q0`'s texel, hence the **plus-side** substitution
`⌊(C + q)/2⌋`, twice as far outside the segment. Column `x` = 8, segment `y` = 7..15: the
region 2 points writing at `u⁻` = 7 read row 23 ≡ **7** (opaque) without `antiShadow` and rows
16, 16, 17 ≡ **0, 0, 1** (transparent) with it. Measured, turning `antiShadow` on alone takes the
row-192 tiles from 524 / 632 vertices to **344 / 464** — the Zaxnes figures exactly.

**The reading.** Each column of the fill takes the texel of **its own** side: the `u⁺` writes
that of `Q1`, the `u⁻` writes that of `Q3`. For the full types this is the text verbatim —
`Q1`'s texel is `Q2`'s and `Q3`'s is `Q0`'s, both pairs being `u⁺` and `u⁻` — so TV-Y4, TV-Y9
and TV-Y10 do not move by one voxel, and TV-Y4's sentence becomes the general statement of the
rule instead of a corollary of the full types. Ticket **Q-101** written, referenced in code.

### Step 4 — Part 2: the fix and its vectors

`src/Zax.Engine3D/VoxelConstruction.cs`, `Quadrants`, the `_fullWarp` branch: the `u⁺` writes
take `t1` and the `u⁻` writes `t3` instead of `t2` and `t0`. For the full types the two namings
are the same value, so that branch is byte-identical there; only the half types move.
