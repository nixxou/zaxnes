# V2-22 — Enhanced style: two conformance defects found in verification (AMD-055)

Lot V2-22 (R3, clean side). The R5 verification found two deviations of the Enhanced style
(AMD-055 v1.3) from the reference behaviour on Punch-Out!! and Super Mario Bros. 1-1:
floor reflections do nothing on the boxing-ring mat, and the Depth ambient occlusion is far
too weak.

## Notes

### Measurement method (same as R5)

Captures downscaled to 960 × 540 (box filter); a pixel counts as "changed by the effect" when
the sum over RGB of |effect − base| exceeds 12. Scene: Punch-Out!! (USA),
profile "Punch Out!!", `--run 1501` with the start presses of the lot, `--size 2560x1440`.
The comparison tool (a ~190-line PNG reader + box downscale + counter) lives in the session
scratchpad, not in the repository. Second scene for the cross-check:
`Super Mario Bros. [Europe][!].nes`, profile "Super Mario Bros.".

### Before (Punch-Out!!, frame 1501, 2560 × 1440)

| capture | changed | reference (R5) |
|---|---|---|
| `spl.reflections` = 1 vs base | **0.00 %** | ≈ 10.5 % |
| `spl.ao` = 2 (Depth) vs base | **5.21 %** | ≈ 23 % |
| `spl.ao` = 1 (MSVO) vs base | 20.59 % | — |

MSVO is therefore already of the right magnitude; the Depth mode is the one that is four times
too weak. The two modes are not under the declared fallback **(D)** — the MiniEngine port landed
in V2-20 — so the invariant of identity between `spl.ao` = 1 and 2 does not apply.

### Defect 1 — diagnosis

Bisected with two throw-away shader probes (reverted; nothing of them is committed):

- forcing `uFloorExtent` to 1 on every opaque item: still **0.00 %** changed;
- forcing `floorFacing` to 1 and keeping the real extent test: **24.81 %** changed.

So the extent half of REQ-SCN-084 accepts the mat and the **normal half** rejects it. A third
probe wrote `uFloorExtent` to the colour buffer: the mat's pixels carry the flag. A fourth wrote
`normalize(vFlatNormal)`: the mat's scene normal, before curvature, is **(0, 0, −1)** — its +Y
component is 0.000, far under the 0.9 of the requirement. No face in the whole frame has
+Y ≥ 0.9; 12.3 % of it carries faces of normal (0, −1, 0) (the stood-up characters and the
near-facing crowd walls).

Cause: the "Punch Out!!" profile runs the **local camera** of REQ-SCN-009 (`localCam True`,
angles (−65, 0, 0)). The NES picture plane is laid flat and is itself the ring mat; the vertical
of the scene is the camera's own, `q · (0, 1, 0)` = (0, 0.4226, **−0.9063**), not the world +Y.
Read literally, REQ-SCN-084's floor definition cannot select such a surface. Per the lot's
instruction this is **not** guessed at: ticket **Q-094**, no code change for defect 1.

### Defect 2 — what was checked and what was found

Checked against REQ-SCN-083 and found **conformant**: `r = (8 + 6 × (ao.radius − 1)) × s` in
scene units, which are view units (`OcclusionRadius`); the ceiling `O ≤ 0.1 × ao.strength`
(`OcclusionCeiling`) and the proportionality to `ao.strength`; `ao.thickness` inert outside
MSVO; the half-resolution buffer except at High; the exact depth linearisation (the inverse
projection on the window depth, not an approximation); the depth-aware blur; and the multiply of
the **whole** lit colour by `1 − O`, in linear space before the sRGB encode, by a `dst × src`
blend.

Two real deviations found and fixed:

1. **The kernel was built for the array, not for the sample count.** `BuildKernel` filled the
   sixteen entries of `uKernel` with a spiral and a square packing over sixteen ranks, while the
   shader reads only the first `uSamples` of them (6 / 10 / 16). At the default quality the ten
   entries read spanned `0.11 r` to `0.45 r` and hugged the pole of the hemisphere: the normative
   **radius of influence r** was simply not covered. Fixed by building the kernel for the count
   the quality reads.
2. **The normal rebuilt from the depth was too noisy.** A one-texel baseline against a 24-bit
   depth buffer tilted the normal enough for **90 of 520** sampled pixels of a lone plane to
   occlude themselves — the first invariant of §2.4 held only because the darkening was weak
   everywhere. Fixed by a two-texel baseline plus a tangent-plane test that replaces the depth
   bias: an occluder must rise above the plane of the point (sine ≥ 0.1) to count, so a surface
   can never darken itself however grazing the view of it.

The remaining gap was the **magnitude**, which the amendment leaves free (only a ceiling and a
proportionality are normative). Ticket **Q-095** records the two readings and the provisional
choice: the raw blocked share is normalised on the right-angle **corner**, measured at 0.35 on
close-up A, so the ceiling is attainable; a simple edge reaches about two thirds of it. The
radial law of the kernel also goes from `rank²` to `rank`.

### After

| scene | effect | before | after | reference (R5) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Punch-Out!! 1501 | `spl.ao` = 2 | 5.21 % | **21.43 %** | ≈ 23 % |
| Punch-Out!! 1501 | `spl.ao` = 1 | 20.59 % | 20.59 % | — |
| Punch-Out!! 1501 | `spl.reflections` = 1 | 0.00 % | 0.00 % | ≈ 10.5 % (Q-094) |
| Super Mario Bros. 1-1 | `spl.ao` = 2 | 0.21 % | **1.52 %** | ≈ 2.0 % |
| Super Mario Bros. 1-1 | `spl.ao` = 1 | 0.48 % | 0.48 % | — |

The 0.21 % measured before the fix on Super Mario Bros. is exactly the 0.2 % R5 reports, which
is what validates the measurement method used here.

MSVO was checked for the same deviation and does **not** have it on the lot's main scene
(20.59 % against a reference of 23 %); its code is untouched. It is however weaker than the
corrected Depth mode on Super Mario Bros. (0.48 % against 1.52 %), with no reference figure in
MSVO to compare against — the subsidiary question of Q-095.

### Regression tests

Both go to `tests/Acceptance`, where the AMD-055 tests of lots V2-18 to V2-20 already live and
whose close-up fixtures they reuse. Each was checked to **fail** on the code before the lot:

- `Q095_the_darkening_at_the_default_settings_is_a_band_and_not_an_outline` — close-up A at the
  default `ao.*`: the contact ratio is in [0.70, 0.90] and the floor is still darkened 4, 10 and
  16 units from the cube's face, the profile never growing with the distance. Fails on 559e856's
  parent, where the darkening dies within a pixel of the contact. The bounds are loose on
  purpose: they say « a band, not an outline », the review criterion of §2.4, and leave the law
  free.
- `Q094_a_flat_mat_under_the_local_camera_is_not_a_floor_of_the_requirement` — the ring-mat
  configuration: a flat multi-tile surface of normal (0, 0, −1), the cube standing on it, seen
  through the local camera of REQ-SCN-009 at the angles of the "Punch Out!!" profile, so that it
  is the floor of the picture. No pixel of it changes, the pass having run. Checked to fail when
  the floor test is moved to the camera's vertical: the mat then carries the red reflection of
  the cube — (113, 82, 82) against (109, 109, 109) — which is the defect R5 reported, reproduced
  in a synthetic scene. **If Q-094 is answered the other way, this is the test to turn round.**
